Camera needs HTTPS
Camera is blocked on local files. Options:
1. Deploy to Railway or Netlify (free HTTPS)
2. Run local server: open Terminal, type:
python3 -m http.server 8080
Then open http://localhost:8080
How it works

1. Mount phone with camera facing the board
2. Tap 2 calibration points: Bull centre, then T20 outer edge
3. Capture a clean baseline (no darts in board)
4. Throw darts then tap Detect
5. Confirm or adjust before submitting

Camera 60-100cm away, slightly to one side
Even lighting gives best results
Keep phone fixed once calibrated
